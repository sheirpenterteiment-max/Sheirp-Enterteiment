Debut Process

Every applicant follows a structured evaluation process designed to identify talent, dedication and potential. Passing one stage does not guarantee debut, but each step brings you closer to becoming a Sheirp Entertainment artist.

1. Online Application

Submit your audition form along with your singing, dancing or rap videos. Our team will carefully review every application.

2. First Evaluation

Applicants who meet our standards will be invited to the next stage for a more detailed talent evaluation.

3. Online Interview

Selected candidates will participate in an online interview to learn more about their goals, personality and commitment.

4. Training Program

Trainees receive training in vocals, dance, rap, stage performance, teamwork and professional development.

5. Monthly Evaluation

Progress is evaluated regularly. Trainees who continue improving advance toward the final stages.

6. Debut Opportunity

When a trainee demonstrates exceptional talent, dedication and readiness, they may be selected to debut as a solo artist or as part of a group.
